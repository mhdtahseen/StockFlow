**The AI-Powered Financial Watchtower for Hardware Resellers and Wholesalers.**

## 💎 The Value Proposition

In the high-velocity hardware market, **precision is profit.** Traditional inventory systems are passive—they wait for you to type in data, leading to human error, missed repair costs, and financial "leakage."

**stockflow** is different. It is an active ecosystem that automates accuracy through AI scanning and an event-driven financial engine.

### For Independent Resellers
- **Speed**: Scan IMEIs in milliseconds, not minutes.
- **Profit Clarity**: Know your *True Profit* after repairs and fees, not just a guess.
- **Mobile First**: Run your entire business from a phone with a desktop-grade analytics engine.

### For Wholesalers
- **Institutional Integrity**: Every unit has a permanent, unalterable audit trail.
- **Unit-Level Economics**: Track costs and margins across thousands of individual IMEIs with zero data-entry friction.
- **Real-Time Sync**: Coordinate between warehouse staff and finance teams instantly.

## ⚡ Key Feature Showcases

### 1. The Smart Scanner HUD
Our proprietary scanning engine uses high-performance OCR and Barcode recognition optimized for the toughest environments.
- **Zero-Blink Technology**: We use a custom "Passive Ref" pattern that keeps the video feed perfectly stable at 60fps—no UI lag, no "black flickers."
- **Low-Light Persistence**: Adaptive thresholding and sharpening kernels ensure IMEIs are captured even in dimly lit warehouses or through reflective packaging.
- **Universal Zoom**: One-handed operation with digital and hardware zoom support.

### 2. The Financial Watchtower
Stop "losing" money to hidden costs. stockflow’s **Automated Ledger** listens to your business operations and logs every penny automatically.
- **Automatic Repair Tracking**: Log a repair, and the ledger instantly deducts it from that unit's margin.
- **PO Refund Automation**: Rejecting a unit during intake? The "Watchtower" automatically creates a refund-due entry for the vendor.
- **Audit-Ready History**: Every ledger entry is linked to a transaction, person, and timestamp.
